The everyday foundation

Build meals around a protein food, carbohydrates, vegetables or fruit, and some fats. Keep water available through the day and around exercise. These examples are optional meal ideas, not calorie targets or a complete daily menu.

Choose ingredients that suit your allergies and dietary needs. For a medical condition, pregnancy or a prescribed diet, ask a qualified dietitian for individual advice.

How your recommendations work

FORM ranks movements using your equipment, level, exercise preferences and recorded history. It retains primary movements, estimates session time including rests, and reports weekly working-set coverage. Load increases require two consistent exposures at the top of the range with at least 2 reps left; unknown effort never triggers an increase. Review upcoming sessions to preview changes before applying them. These are transparent planning rules, not a guarantee of optimal results. The app cannot assess technique or health.

General guidance for adults. Start gradually, use controlled movements and stop if an exercise causes pain. A qualified coach can check unfamiliar lifts. Rest days leave space for recovery, easy walks or mobility.
